# Risk Contribution

## Definitions
Marginal risk contribution:
`MCR_i = (Sigma w)_i / sigma_p`

Component risk contribution:
`CR_i = w_i * MCR_i`

## Identity
`sum_i CR_i = sigma_p`

## Why PMs care
Capital weights and risk weights can be radically different.
