# Tracking Error

## One-line definition
Volatility of portfolio returns relative to the benchmark.

## Formula
Let active weights be `a = w_p - w_b`.

`TE = sqrt(a^T Sigma a)`

## Why PMs care
It is a central measure of benchmark-relative risk and active aggressiveness.

## Common mistake
Confusing total portfolio volatility with tracking error.
